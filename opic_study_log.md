### **[Question 1: Description]**
**Q: Tell me about a concert hall or a venue where you like to go to listen to music. What does it look like, and why do you like going there? Provide as many details as possible.**

### **[Question 2: Role-play]**
**Q: You want to sign up for personal training sessions at a local gym. Call the gym and ask three or four questions to get the information you need.**
**(동네 체육관에서 퍼스널 트레이닝(PT)을 등록하려고 합니다. 체육관에 전화하여 필요한 정보를 얻기 위해 3~4가지 질문을 하세요.)**

#### **[Model Answer]**
Hi there! I’m calling because I’m interested in signing up for some personal training sessions at your gym. I saw your advertisement online and <strong style="color:blue;">checked out</strong> your website, but I have a few specific questions before I <strong style="color:blue;">drop by</strong> to register. 

First of all, I was wondering about the background of your trainers. I’m currently recovering from a minor knee injury, so I’d like to know if you have any trainers who specialize in rehabilitation or corrective exercise. It’s really important for me to <strong style="color:blue;">work out</strong> safely without making my injury worse. 

Secondly, could you <strong style="color:blue;">fill me in on</strong> the pricing plans? I saw that you offer packages for 10 or 20 sessions, but is there any discount if I sign up for a long-term membership at the same time? Also, do you provide a free trial session so I can <strong style="color:blue;">feel out</strong> the training style before committing?

Lastly, I have a pretty hectic work schedule, so I’m curious about the operating hours. Do you have trainers available early in the morning, say around 6 AM, or late at night? It would be great if you could <strong style="color:blue;">walk me through</strong> the booking process as well. Thanks in advance, and I <strong style="color:blue;">look forward to</strong> hearing from you!

#### **[Vocabulary & Expressions]**
*   **Rehabilitation:** 재활
*   **Corrective exercise:** 교정 운동
*   **Hectic:** 정신없이 바쁜
*   **Checked out:** ~을 확인하다/살펴보다
*   **Drop by:** 잠깐 들르다
*   **Work out:** 운동하다
*   **Fill someone in on:** ~에 대해 자세히 알려주다
*   **Feel out:** (상황 등을) 살피다/알아보다
*   **Walk someone through:** ~을 차근차근 설명해주다
*   **Look forward to:** ~을 고대하다

### **[Question 3: Unexpected/Issue]**
**Q: Technology has changed how people communicate. Compare how people communicated in the past with how they communicate now. What are the pros and cons of these changes?**
**(기술은 사람들이 소통하는 방식을 변화시켰습니다. 과거와 현재의 소통 방식을 비교해 보세요. 이러한 변화의 장단점은 무엇인가요?)**

#### **[Model Answer]**
That’s a very intriguing question. It’s undeniable that the way we <strong style="color:blue;">keep in touch</strong> with one another has undergone a massive transformation due to rapid technological advancements. 

In the past, communication was much slower and required more effort. I remember my parents telling me about writing handwritten letters or having to <strong style="color:blue;">line up</strong> at a public phone booth just to <strong style="color:blue;">get through to</strong> someone. Back then, people valued each interaction more because it wasn't as instant. 

Fast forward to today, and everything is <strong style="color:blue;">at the tip of our fingers</strong>. With smartphones and social media platforms, we can <strong style="color:blue;">reach out to</strong> anyone across the globe in a matter of seconds. We can share photos, videos, and even our live locations instantly. This has certainly made our lives more convenient and has helped us <strong style="color:blue;">stay caught up with</strong> our friends’ lives.

However, I believe this is a <strong style="color:blue;">double-edged sword</strong>. While we are more connected than ever, the quality of our conversations seems to have <strong style="color:blue;">died down</strong>. People often <strong style="color:blue;">end up</strong> scrolling through their phones even when they are sitting right across from each other. In other words, physical presence doesn’t always guarantee meaningful connection anymore. 

In conclusion, while technology has <strong style="color:blue;">broken down</strong> geographical barriers, we need to be careful not to let it <strong style="color:blue;">get in the way of</strong> genuine, face-to-face human interaction. We should strive to find a balance between digital convenience and real-life intimacy.

#### **[Vocabulary & Expressions]**
*   **Undergone a transformation:** 변화를 겪다
*   **Instant:** 즉각적인
*   **Geographical barriers:** 지리적 장벽
*   **Intimacy:** 친밀함
*   **Keep in touch:** 연락을 유지하다
*   **Line up:** 줄을 서다
*   **Get through to:** ~에게 연락이 닿다
*   **At the tip of one's fingers:** 아주 가까이에/손쉽게 이용 가능한
*   **Reach out to:** ~에게 연락을 취하다
*   **Stay caught up with:** ~의 최신 소식을 계속 파악하다
*   **Double-edged sword:** 양날의 검
*   **Die down:** 서서히 가라앉다/약해지다
*   **End up:** 결국 ~하게 되다
*   **Broken down:** (장벽 등을) 허물다
*   **Get in the way of:** ~을 방해하다
